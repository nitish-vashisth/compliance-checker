| #      | Stage                                   | Prompt                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Input                                              | Output                                         | Suggested file                          |
| ------ | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **1**  | **Source Registration**                 | **Register this regulatory source. Identify its authority, jurisdiction, framework, version, status and canonical URL. Do not interpret or extract requirements.**                                                                                                                                              | Source URL / source metadata                       | Registered source definition                   | `01-source-registration.json`           |
| **2**  | **Source Discovery**                    | **Discover the authoritative sources that belong to this regulatory source family. Identify primary sources, official guidance, definitions, structured data and supporting government sources. Do not extract regulatory rules yet. Do not invent sources.**                                                   | Step 1 output + source URL                         | Source family + authoritative source inventory | `02-source-discovery.json`              |
| **3**  | **Source Understanding**                | **Read the identified authoritative sources and create a factual representation of what each source contains. Extract only source-supported facts. Every fact must have a source reference. Do not infer customer applicability, generate rules, generate signals or create requirements.**                     | Step 2 source inventory + retrieved source content | Traceable source facts / concepts              | `03-source-understanding.json`          |
| **4**  | **Regulatory Knowledge Extraction**     | **Transform the verified source facts into structured regulatory knowledge. Identify actors, scope, regulated objects, activities, conditions, obligations, exceptions, definitions, relationships and dependencies. Preserve source traceability. Do not determine applicability to a hypothetical customer.** | Step 3 verified source facts                       | Regulatory knowledge model                     | `04-regulatory-knowledge.json`          |
| **5**  | **Candidate Signal Discovery**          | **Determine what observable customer facts would be needed to evaluate the regulatory knowledge. Identify candidate signals and explain which regulatory fact each signal supports. Do not decide the customer's values.**                                                                                      | Step 4 regulatory knowledge                        | Candidate customer signals                     | `05-candidate-signals.json`             |
| **6**  | **Derived Concept Generation**          | **Identify concepts that can be deterministically derived from one or more customer signals. Define the derivation logic and distinguish derived concepts from direct customer-provided facts.**                                                                                                                | Step 5 candidate signals + Step 4 knowledge        | Derived concepts + derivation definitions      | `06-derived-concepts.json`              |
| **7**  | **Applicability Rule Generation**       | **Generate deterministic applicability rules from the regulatory knowledge, candidate signals and derived concepts. Every rule must have a traceable regulatory basis. Identify rules requiring customer information and rules that can be evaluated deterministically.**                                       | Steps 4–6                                          | Applicability rules                            | `07-applicability-rules.json`           |
| **8**  | **Requirement / Obligation Extraction** | **Extract the obligations and requirements associated with applicable regulatory concepts. Preserve the distinction between legal/regulatory obligations, recommendations, guidance and technical controls. Do not map them to a specific product yet.**                                                        | Step 4 knowledge + Step 7 rules                    | Requirements / obligations                     | `08-requirements.json`                  |
| **9**  | **Exception / Boundary Extraction**     | **Identify exceptions, exclusions, thresholds, boundaries and conditions that can change applicability or obligations. Convert them into explicit machine-readable conditions where possible.**                                                                                                                 | Steps 4, 7, 8                                      | Exceptions / boundaries                        | `09-exceptions-boundaries.json`         |
| **10** | **Evidence Model Generation**           | **For every signal, derived concept and applicability rule, define what evidence can establish its value, the acceptable evidence source, evidence quality and confidence.**                                                                                                                                    | Steps 5–9                                          | Evidence definitions                           | `10-evidence-model.json`                |
| **11** | **Discovery Question Generation**       | **Generate the minimum customer questions required to resolve unknown signals that materially affect applicability or requirements. Each question must identify the signal/rule it resolves and why it is needed.**                                                                                             | Steps 5–10                                         | Discovery questions                            | `11-discovery-questions.json`           |
| **12** | **High-Value Signal Selection**         | **Select the smallest set of customer signals/questions that provides sufficient information to evaluate the regulatory rules. Prioritize signals by decision impact and avoid unnecessary questions.**                                                                                                         | Steps 5–11                                         | Optimized assessment signal set                | `12-assessment-signals.json`            |
| **13** | **Assessment Profile Generation**       | **Create the machine-readable assessment profile for this framework. Define required inputs, optional inputs, derived values, evidence and rules needed to evaluate applicability.**                                                                                                                            | Steps 5–12                                         | Assessment profile                             | `13-assessment-profile.json`            |
| **14** | **Customer Input Template**             | **Create the customer-facing input schema required by the assessment profile. Every field must map to a known signal and regulatory decision.**                                                                                                                                                                 | Step 13                                            | Customer questionnaire/input schema            | `14-customer-input.json`                |
| **15** | **Validation**                          | **Validate the complete regulatory knowledge pack for traceability, consistency, missing dependencies, unsupported claims, conflicting rules and unsupported inference. Reject anything that cannot be traced to authoritative source material.**                                                               | Steps 1–14                                         | Validation report + pass/fail                  | `15-validation.json`                    |
| **16** | **Test Scenario Generation**            | **Generate synthetic customer scenarios that exercise every applicability rule, exception, boundary and unknown-information path. Do not use real customer data.**                                                                                                                                              | Steps 7–15                                         | Test cases                                     | `16-test-scenarios.json`                |
| **17** | **Requirement Pack Assembly**           | **Assemble the validated regulatory knowledge into a versioned framework requirement pack that can be consumed by the assessment engine. Preserve complete traceability.**                                                                                                                                      | Steps 1–16                                         | Final regulatory pack                          | `17-regulatory-pack.json`               |
| **18** | **Atlassian Source Registration**       | **Register the authoritative Atlassian security/compliance sources that will be used for product capability mapping. Do not make regulatory applicability decisions.**                                                                                                                                          | Atlassian source URLs                              | Atlassian source inventory                     | `18-atlassian-source-registration.json` |
| **19** | **Atlassian Knowledge Extraction**      | **Extract verifiable Atlassian capabilities, limitations, prerequisites and relevant controls from authoritative Atlassian sources. Preserve source traceability.**                                                                                                                                             | Step 18 + Atlassian source content                 | Atlassian capability knowledge                 | `19-atlassian-knowledge.json`           |
| **20** | **Regulatory → Atlassian Mapping**      | **Map validated regulatory requirements to relevant Atlassian capabilities. Clearly distinguish supported, partially supported, unsupported and unknown capabilities. Do not claim compliance solely because a capability exists.**                                                                             | Steps 17 + 19                                      | Requirement-to-capability mapping              | `20-atlassian-mapping.json`             |
| **21** | **Customer Assessment Execution**       | **Evaluate the customer input against the validated applicability rules. Execute deterministic rules and record evidence, unknowns and rule outcomes. Do not invent missing customer information.**                                                                                                             | Customer input + Step 17 pack                      | Rule evaluation results                        | `21-assessment-result.json`             |
| **22** | **Missing Information Analysis**        | **Identify only the missing customer information that can materially change an applicability or requirement outcome. Rank the missing information by decision impact.**                                                                                                                                         | Step 21 result                                     | Missing information                            | `22-missing-information.json`           |
| **23** | **Adaptive Discovery**                  | **Generate the next minimum set of customer questions required to resolve the highest-impact unknowns. Stop asking questions when additional answers cannot materially improve the assessment.**                                                                                                                | Step 22 + rules                                    | Follow-up questions                            | `23-adaptive-questions.json`            |
| **24** | **Final Applicability Assessment**      | **Produce the final applicability determination for each regulatory concept, including status, evidence, confidence, reasoning, unresolved unknowns and source references.**                                                                                                                                    | Customer answers + Steps 17, 21–23                 | Final regulatory assessment                    | `24-final-assessment.json`              |
| **25** | **Atlassian Readiness Assessment**      | **Evaluate the applicable requirements against Atlassian capabilities and customer-specific needs. Identify supported capabilities, gaps, dependencies, unknowns and required validation.**                                                                                                                     | Step 24 + Step 20                                  | Atlassian readiness assessment                 | `25-atlassian-readiness.json`           |
| **26** | **Pre-Sales Readiness Result**          | **Produce the final pre-sales readiness assessment with applicable regulations, requirements, customer evidence, confidence, missing information, Atlassian capability mapping, gaps and recommended discovery actions.**                                                                                       | Steps 24–25                                        | Final readiness brief                          | `26-pre-sales-readiness.json`           |
| **27** | **Human Review**                        | **Present all low-confidence, ambiguous, conflicting or legally significant findings requiring human review. Do not silently resolve uncertainty.**                                                                                                                                                             | Step 26 + validation metadata                      | Human review queue                             | `27-human-review.json`                  |
